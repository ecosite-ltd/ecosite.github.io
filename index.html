Our Mission

Transforming the way commercial solar is deployed across the UK

The Problem

Commercial solar deployment is held back by inefficient prospecting. Sales teams waste countless hours trawling Google Maps for suitable rooftops, manually assessing sites one by one, often pursuing unsuitable leads while missing high-potential opportunities.

Why It Matters

Every day of delay means lost carbon savings, higher customer acquisition costs, and slower clean energy deployment. The UK has enormous untapped potential for commercial solar — the opportunity is there, but the prospecting bottleneck is holding the industry back.

Our Solution

Now PV uses GenAI-powered satellite imagery analysis to identify and qualify commercial solar opportunities across entire postcode districts in minutes. From AI roof analysis to design tool integration, we streamline the entire prospecting workflow.
